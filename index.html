What Technology Do You Think Will Be Obsolete in The Next Few Years?

Date

When talking about what technology will become obsolete or useless in the next few years. I think that smart home devices will become obsolete in the future, mostly because people would rather just type it into their phone or speak to their phone directly, without buying a whole device just for that alone. I personally have only ever used one as a glorified speaker, as I had to connect my phone to it regardless, and I don't see anyone mentioning them as much as they used to be advertised. I think that something that is already starting to be more common is individual smart technology that is directly connected to your phone with apps, bulbs, microwaves, fridges, etc. It is a lot more convenient to use your phone as a remote instead of having to shout at a little device that gets used once in a while.
